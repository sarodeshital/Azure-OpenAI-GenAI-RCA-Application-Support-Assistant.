# Incident RCA Procedure

## Standard Support Lifecycle

1. Acknowledge incident.
2. Classify incident.
3. Determine business impact.
4. Collect logs and metrics.
5. Establish timeline.
6. Isolate failing component.
7. Form root-cause hypothesis.
8. Correlate hypothesis with evidence.
9. Apply lowest-risk mitigation.
10. Validate recovery.
11. Document RCA.
12. Define preventive action.
